Data

The widespread and everchanging nature of the political landscape makes it difficult to study all political nominations. As such, we have limited our analysis to Trump's first 300 days in office and will only compare the first 300 days of other presidencies. We have also decided to focus on nominations made to the 15 executive departments in the presidential line of succession - State, Treasury, Defense, Justice, Interior, Agriculture, Commerce, Labor, Health and Human Services, Housing and Urban Development, Transportation, Energy, Education, Veterans Affairs, and Homeland Security - as these positions are the most politically significant and had the most available data.

Data from this project was taken from the Partnership for Public Service and The Washington Post's Political Appointee Tracker, as well as the Brookings Institution. Data was collected on November 17, 2025 and does not cover nominations after that date. It has information on all of Joe Biden and Trump’s second term appointees in the first 300 days, including their name, position, race/ethnicity, gender, the date they were confirmed, and their confirmation period (0-100 days, 101-200 days, or 201-300 days). We hard-coded some of the unknown data on race and standardized race categories.

Colors were adapted from colorblind-friendly palette devised by Masataka Okabe (Jikei Medical School) and Kei Ito (University of Tokyo).
